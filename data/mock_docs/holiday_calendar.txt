Holiday Calendar – 2026

GuardRail Corp observes 12 paid public holidays each calendar year, including Republic Day (26 January), Independence Day (15 August), Gandhi Jayanti (2 October) and Diwali. Employees may also choose 2 floating holidays from the optional list that HR publishes each December.

A holiday that falls on a weekend is not carried forward. Customer support teams follow a holiday rotation roster approved by their Reporting Manager.

Questions about the holiday calendar can be sent to hr.ops@guardrailcorp.com.
