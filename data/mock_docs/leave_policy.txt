GuardRail Corp — Employee Leave Policy

All full-time employees are entitled to 18 days of paid annual leave per
calendar year, accrued monthly. Leave requests must be submitted through
the HR portal at least 5 working days in advance for planned leave.

Sick leave is separate from annual leave and covers up to 12 days per
year with a medical certificate required for absences longer than 2
consecutive days.

New employees begin accruing leave from their date of joining, but
cannot use accrued leave during their first 90 days of employment
(probation period), except for emergency sick leave.

Managers are responsible for approving or rejecting leave requests
within 2 working days of submission.
