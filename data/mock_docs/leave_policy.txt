Leave Policy – GuardRail Corp

All full-time employees are entitled to 18 days of paid annual leave and 12 days of casual leave per calendar year. Sick leave of up to 10 days is available with valid medical documentation.

Leave requests must be submitted through the HRMS portal at least 5 working days in advance. In case of emergencies, employees may apply retrospectively with manager approval.

For leave balance or policy-related queries, contact HR Operations Executive Ananya Reddy (ananya.reddy@guardrailcorp.com).
