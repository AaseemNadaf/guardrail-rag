Vendor Management Policy

All new vendors must be onboarded through the procurement portal and approved by the Procurement team before any purchase order is issued. Vendors that handle company data must sign a data-processing agreement and complete a security questionnaire.

Purchases above ₹2,00,000 require three competitive quotations. Invoices are paid within 45 days of receipt, subject to approval by the requesting department.

For vendor onboarding, contact Procurement Lead Nikhil Bansal (nikhil.bansal@guardrailcorp.com, 022-4567-8901).
