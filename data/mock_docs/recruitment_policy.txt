Recruitment & Hiring Policy

All open positions must be approved by the respective Department Head and HR. Hiring managers are required to follow the structured interview process consisting of technical, managerial, and HR rounds.

Offer letters are issued only after background verification. Salary offers must remain within the approved compensation band unless special approval is obtained from the CHRO.
