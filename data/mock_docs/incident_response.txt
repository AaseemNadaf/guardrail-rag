Incident Response Procedure

All security or data incidents must be reported immediately to the Incident Response Team. The team will assess severity and initiate containment steps.

Employees should not attempt to investigate or delete evidence on their own. Detailed logs of the incident response process are maintained for audit purposes.

For escalation, contact Incident Lead Rohan Verma (rohan.verma@guardrailcorp.com, +91-98765-43210) or email security-incident@guardrailcorp.com.
