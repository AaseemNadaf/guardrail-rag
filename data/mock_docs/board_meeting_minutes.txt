Board Meeting Minutes – Q2 FY 2026-27 (Restricted)

The Board reviewed the proposed acquisition of Veridian Analytics under the codename Project Falcon. The indicative valuation is ₹420 crore, subject to due diligence. The Board approved a non-binding term sheet and authorised the CFO to appoint legal and financial advisers.

The Board also approved a 9% increase in the annual merit budget. Details of the acquisition must not be disclosed until a public announcement is made.
