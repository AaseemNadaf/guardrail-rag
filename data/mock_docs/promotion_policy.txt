Promotion & Career Progression Policy

Promotions are considered during the bi-annual review cycle based on sustained performance, impact, and readiness for the next role. A minimum rating of 4 is generally required.

Promotion recommendations must be supported by the Reporting Manager and approved by the Department Head and HR. Compensation changes linked to promotion will follow the approved salary bands.

For questions regarding promotion eligibility, contact Priya Sharma (priya.sharma@guardrailcorp.com) from the Compensation team.
