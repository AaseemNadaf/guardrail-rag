Employee Exit / Separation Policy

Employees resigning from GuardRail Corp must serve a notice period of 60 days (or as mentioned in the appointment letter). Clearance from IT, Finance, and Reporting Manager is mandatory before full and final settlement.

Exit interviews are conducted by HR. All company assets, documents, and access credentials must be returned on or before the last working day.

For exit formalities, employees should coordinate with HR Executive Karan Malhotra (karan.malhotra@guardrailcorp.com), Employee ID: GC-HR-1156.
