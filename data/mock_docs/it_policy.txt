IT Policy – GuardRail Corp

All employees must use company-approved devices and software for official work. Installation of unauthorized applications is strictly prohibited. Employees are required to keep their systems updated and run regular antivirus scans.

Access to internal systems must be done through the company VPN when working remotely. Sharing of login credentials is not allowed under any circumstances.

For IT support or access-related issues, employees can contact the IT Helpdesk at it.support@guardrailcorp.com or reach out to IT Support Lead Arjun Desai (arjun.desai@guardrailcorp.com).
