GuardRail Corp — Engineering Onboarding Guide

Welcome to the engineering team. Your first week should cover the
following setup steps: request access to the internal GitLab instance,
set up your local development environment using the team's Docker
Compose stack, and complete the mandatory security training module.

All engineers are expected to follow the internal code review process:
every merge request requires at least one approval from a senior
engineer before merging to the main branch.

The on-call rotation is managed through PagerDuty. New engineers join
the on-call rotation only after their first 60 days, once they have
shadowed at least two incidents with a senior engineer.

For questions about internal tooling, post in the #eng-help Slack
channel rather than emailing individual team members directly.
