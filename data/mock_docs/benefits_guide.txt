Employee Benefits Guide

GuardRail Corp provides a comprehensive benefits package that includes group medical insurance (employee + family up to ₹5 lakhs), group term life insurance, provident fund, and gratuity as per statutory requirements.

Employees are also eligible for a Learning & Development allowance of ₹25,000 per year. Mental health support is available through the company’s partner platform.

For enrollment, claims, or benefit-related questions, employees can reach out to Benefits Coordinator Ritu Sharma (ritu.sharma@guardrailcorp.com).
