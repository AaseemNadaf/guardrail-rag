Performance Review Process

GuardRail Corp follows a bi-annual performance review cycle (April–September and October–March). Employees are evaluated on goal achievement, technical contribution, collaboration, and ownership.

Ratings range from 1 (Needs Improvement) to 5 (Outstanding). A rating of 4 or above is required for promotion consideration. Managers must complete reviews within 15 days of the cycle end date.

Performance-related discussions are confidential. For process-related questions, reach out to HR Business Partner Neha Kapoor (neha.kapoor@guardrailcorp.com), Employee ID: GC-HR-1098.
