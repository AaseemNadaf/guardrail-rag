Attendance & Working Hours Policy

Standard working hours at GuardRail Corp are 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM IST, Monday to Friday. Employees are expected to mark their attendance regularly through the designated system.

Frequent late arrivals or unplanned absences will be reviewed by the Reporting Manager. Flexible timings may be allowed with prior approval, provided core working hours are covered.

For attendance regularization or related queries, contact HR Operations at hr.ops@guardrailcorp.com.
