Workplace Health and Safety

All employees must know the emergency exits on their floor. Fire drills are conducted every quarter, and attendance is mandatory for everyone present in the office that day.

First-aid kits are kept at each reception desk, and trained first-aid volunteers are listed on the floor notice board. Any workplace injury, however minor, must be reported within 24 hours.

For safety concerns, contact Facilities Manager Kavya Menon (kavya.menon@guardrailcorp.com, +91-90110-22334).
