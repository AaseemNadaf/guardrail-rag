Payroll Processing Schedule (Confidential)

Salaries are credited on the last working day of each month. The payroll cut-off is the 20th of the month; attendance or reimbursement changes submitted after the cut-off are processed in the following cycle.

Payroll is run by the Payroll Control Desk on the PayGrid system. Bank transfer files are released to the salary account partner two working days before the credit date. Off-cycle payments require approval from the Finance Controller.
