Workforce Restructuring Plan – Project Northwind (Confidential)

Under Project Northwind, the Customer Support function will be consolidated from three locations into the Pune office by the end of Q3 FY 2026-27. Approximately 18 positions in Hyderabad are expected to be impacted.

Impacted employees will be offered an internal transfer first. Those who do not transfer will receive a separation package of 3 months' gross salary in addition to statutory dues.

The plan must not be communicated to employees before the official announcement. Queries should be directed to HR Business Partner Rahul Khanna (rahul.khanna@guardrailcorp.com).
