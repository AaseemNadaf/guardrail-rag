Data Classification & Handling Policy

Company data is classified as Public, Internal, Confidential, or Restricted. Restricted data (including salary information, personal employee records, and security configurations) must not be shared without explicit authorization.

Access to Restricted data is limited to authorized personnel only. Violations of data handling rules may result in disciplinary action.

For guidance on data classification, contact Information Security Officer Sneha Nair (sneha.nair@guardrailcorp.com), Employee ID: GC-SEC-1021.
