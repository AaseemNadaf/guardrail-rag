Expense Reimbursement Policy

Employees can claim official expenses through the expense management portal within 30 days of incurring the expense. Original bills and manager approval are mandatory for processing.

Reimbursable expenses include local conveyance, official travel, internet allowance (for remote work days), and client-related costs within policy limits. Claims without proper documentation will be rejected.

For queries related to reimbursement status or policy clarification, contact Finance Executive Sneha Kulkarni (sneha.kulkarni@guardrailcorp.com).
