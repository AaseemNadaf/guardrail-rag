Code of Conduct

All employees of GuardRail Corp are expected to uphold the highest standards of integrity, professionalism, and mutual respect. Any form of discrimination, harassment, or unethical behavior will not be tolerated.

Employees must protect confidential company information and avoid situations that may create a conflict of interest. Violations of the Code of Conduct may result in disciplinary action, including termination of employment.

For reporting concerns, employees may write to ethics@guardrailcorp.com.
